## Webinar: The Sampling Event Horizon: Lessons from the Publishing Frontier

This webinar was presented on 22 October 2026, as the third part of the GBIF North America 2026 Nodes Meeting and Training.
 
**Presenters:**
- **Chandra Earl**, NEON Biorepository, Arizona State University
- **Zachary KachianS**, Field Museum of Natural History, Keller Science Action Center 
- **Kate Webbink**, Field Museum of Natural History
- **Kate Ingenloff**, GBIF
- **Steven Formel**, OBIS Data Manager

**Topics covered:**
- Shared lessons learned. 
- Reflections on the changing protocol, bottlenecks and time savers. 
- Practical advice for planning new efforts or retrofitting legacy data into this structure.
- Tools that exist to assist in publishing high-quality biodiversity data from a sampling context.
