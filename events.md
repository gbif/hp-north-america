## GBIF North America: Connecting People, Inspiring Discovery, and Celebrating Biodiversity
At the heart of every GBIF North America event is a simple but powerful idea: when we share knowledge, we create possibilities. These gatherings transform individual expertise into collective inspiration, connecting people, ideas, and data in ways that extend far beyond the event itself. They encourage curiosity, nurture collaboration, build capacity, and remind us that behind every biodiversity record is a story waiting to be discovered. Together, we are not simply sharing data; we are building bridges between science and society, strengthening the foundations for informed decisions, and helping create a future where biodiversity is better known, valued, and protected.

Connecting minds. Sharing knowledge. Celebrating life. Together, we make biodiversity count.
