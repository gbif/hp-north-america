## Webinar: The Sampling Event Horizon: Lessons from the Publishing Frontier

This webinar was presented on 22 October 2026, as the third part of the GBIF North America 2026 Nodes Meeting and Training.
 
**Presenters:**
- **Chandra Earl**, NEON Biorepository, Arizona State University, Biodiversity Informatician
- **Zachary KachianS**, Field Museum of Natural History, Keller Science Action Center, Sr. Data Management Specialist 
- **Kate Webbink**, Field Museum of Natural History, Information Technology, Data Engineer
- **Kate Ingenloff**, GBIF, Scientific Officer
- **Steven Formel**, OBIS Secretariat, Data Officer

**Topics covered:**
- Shared lessons learned from working within more complex event-based data
- Reflections on implementing new data models, challenges and time savers. 
- Practical advice for planning new efforts or retrofitting legacy data into this structure.
- The long-term value of preserving sampling context for broader ecological analysis.
- Resources that exist to assist in publishing high-quality biodiversity data from a sampling context.
