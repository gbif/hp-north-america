Ready to take your biodiversity data beyond simple occurrence records?

Join the next GBIF North America Webinar to learn about how publishing biodiversity data as Sampling Events unlocks richer, more reusable datasets. Pioneers from the GBIF community share their experience crossing the Sampling Event Horizon: once your data crosses into Sampling Event structure, can you go back to simple occurrence records?

In this panel, leaders and stalwart adventurers from the GBIF Secretariat and GBIF North America share lessons learned: the gotchas that cost them time, the bottlenecks that slowed them down, and practical advice for planning new efforts or retrofitting legacy data into this structure.

Please come with questions! After short talks from each panelist, we'll open up to a Q&A/panel discussion covering whatever the sampling event frontier throws at us.
